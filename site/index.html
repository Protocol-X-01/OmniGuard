Built for inspection

Keep the limits visible.

These are synthetic fixture measurements, not production precision or recall. Explicit-super dispatch and one composed-modifier case remain incomplete. The combined defence fixture also labels ten undetected properties. Bitbucket fixture scans have separate production acceptance; encrypted communications remain a local lab.

Bundles include full selected source. Forge retains scan/history records without automatic expiry. New imports do not store importer account IDs; project administrators can remove that field from older scans. This control preserves source and remediation history.
